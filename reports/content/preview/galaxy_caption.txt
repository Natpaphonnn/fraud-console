Transaction galaxy

16,000 dev transactions. Each point is one transaction's 24 per-feature
contributions to the logistic model's log-odds, projected to 3-D with PCA
(52% of the variance). Amber: fraud (271); blue: legitimate. Axes are unitless.

Data: Credit Card Fraud Detection 2026 (Udit Jain, CC0), unspecified provenance.
